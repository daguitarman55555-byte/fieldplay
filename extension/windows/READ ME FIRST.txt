FIELDPLAY FOR DESMOS - ONE-TIME SETUP

1. Move this entire "FieldPlay for Desmos" folder somewhere permanent,
   such as your Documents folder. Do not load it directly from Downloads.

2. Open Chrome and enter this address:
   chrome://extensions

3. Turn on Developer mode in the upper-right corner.

4. Click Load unpacked.

5. Select the "extension" folder beside this file.

UPDATING LATER

Double-click "Update FieldPlay.cmd".
When Chrome's Extensions page opens, click the circular Reload button on
FieldPlay for Desmos. Refresh any open Desmos tab.

You do not need PowerShell, npm, or another ZIP for future updates.
